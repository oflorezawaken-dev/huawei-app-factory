PlantCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 (version 1.0)

Summary: PlantCue does not require an account and does not send your plants, photos or care history to any server operated by us. Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and the app does not back that data up to any cloud. PlantCue is free and shows ads served by Huawei Petal Ads on two screens; the ad SDK uses the network and may collect device and usage data for ad delivery.

What PlantCue stores

  • Your plants: name, room, notes, and the care schedules you set (water, fertilise, mist).
  • Plant photos you take or choose, downscaled and saved only in the app's private storage.
  • Care history: each time you mark something done or snoozed, with the date.
  • Preferences: the reminder hour and your theme choice.

None of this is transmitted to us. We do not operate any server that receives it. There is no user account, sign-in, or profile of any kind. PlantCue sets allowBackup=false, so Android's device backup does not copy the app's data either.

Notifications

PlantCue asks for permission to post notifications so it can send one daily reminder listing the plants that need care. The reminder is generated on your device from your own schedules; no data leaves the device to produce it. You can turn it off any time in your system settings.

Photos and camera

When you choose "Take photo", PlantCue opens your device's camera app and receives only the picture you accept. PlantCue does not request the camera permission itself and never accesses the camera in the background. Photos chosen from your gallery are copied into the app's private storage; the original in your gallery is not modified.

Advertising (Huawei Petal Ads)

PlantCue is free and supported by advertising. Ads are served by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK and appear only on the Plants and Stats screens — never on Today, while adding or editing a plant, or in a session opened from a reminder notification.

To deliver and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (OAID), device model and OS version, network and carrier information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. This information is processed by Huawei under its own privacy policy; PlantCue itself does not receive or store it. Your plants, photos and care history are never shared with the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization on Huawei devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy, and on other Android devices under the equivalent "Ads" setting. For Huawei's own disclosures, see the Petal Ads documentation.

Data deletion

Deleting a plant removes its photo, schedules and history from the device. Uninstalling PlantCue removes all of its data.

Children's privacy

PlantCue is a general-purpose reminder tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case we do not collect personal information on any server.

Changes to this policy

If what PlantCue collects or does changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version is made available.
