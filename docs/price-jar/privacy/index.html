PriceJar Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 (version 1.0.0)

Summary: PriceJar does not require an account and has no server of its own. Every item, price, store, shopping list and trip you enter is stored only in the app's private storage on this iPhone. PriceJar is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob on four browsing screens; the AdMob SDK uses the network and, with your permission, the advertising identifier, to serve and measure those ads.

What PriceJar stores, and where

Everything below lives only in this app's private SwiftData store on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us, and PriceJar has no account, sign-in or server that could receive it.

  • Items: name, brand, category, package size, unit, an optional barcode and an optional photo you choose with the system photo picker.
  • Price entries: the price you record, the store, the date, the package size and unit, and whether it was a sale or loyalty-card price.
  • Stores: the names and notes you give the stores you shop at.
  • Shopping lists and trips: what you planned to buy, and what a saved trip actually cost.
  • Settings: your chosen currency, measurement system, and whether sale/loyalty prices count toward your "typical price".

PriceJar does not look up products, prices or barcodes online. The barcode scanner only recalls items you have already named yourself; if it does not recognise a code, it asks you to add the item, it never guesses a name or price for you.

Exporting or deleting your data

Settings lets you export everything as a CSV file through the system share sheet, remove the sample data offered on first run, or permanently delete every item, price, store and list on the device (typing DELETE to confirm). Uninstalling PriceJar removes all of its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

PriceJar is free and supported by advertising served through Google's AdMob SDK. An anchored banner appears only on the Price Book, Item History, Stores and Stats screens, and at most one full-screen ad can appear after you finish and save a shopping trip. No ad of any kind appears while you are entering a price, using the barcode scanner, viewing the "Good Price?" verdict, adding or editing an item, in Settings, during First Run, or at any point while a shopping trip is active — on the assumption that you may be standing in a shop, one-handed, with your attention elsewhere.

To request permission for cross-app tracking, PriceJar shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once, shortly after you save your first price — never at launch and never during onboarding. If you decline, or on any iPhone where Limit Ad Tracking is otherwise in effect, AdMob serves non-personalised ads instead; every feature of the app keeps working exactly the same way. When tracking is authorised, the AdMob SDK reads the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and reports ad impressions, clicks and the placement they occurred in to Google, so that future ads can be more relevant. PriceJar itself does not read the IDFA and does not send your items, prices, stores, lists or trips to Google or to any other third party.

A one-time Remove Ads purchase, processed by Apple through StoreKit, permanently turns off every ad. PriceJar receives only the entitlement that the purchase happened; it does not receive or store any payment or billing information, which is handled entirely by Apple.

For Google's own disclosures about AdMob, see Google's advertising policy and how Google uses information from sites and apps that use AdMob.

Permissions

  • Camera — requested only the first time you open the barcode scanner, to read a barcode's numbers. PriceJar does not take or save photographs through the scanner. If you deny camera access, everything else keeps working: you can still search the price book by name, pick an item from the list, or type a barcode in by hand.
  • Photos — item photos are chosen through the system's PhotosPicker, which never grants PriceJar access to your photo library as a whole; it only receives the single image you pick.

Children's privacy

PriceJar is a general-purpose shopping tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and PriceJar itself collects no personal information on any server in any case.

Changes to this policy

If what PriceJar collects or does changes, this page and the "Last updated" date above will be updated before that version is made available.
