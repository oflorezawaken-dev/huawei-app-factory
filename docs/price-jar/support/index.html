PriceJar Support

Last updated: September 6, 2026 (version 1.0.0)

Everything in PriceJar lives on your iPhone. There is no account to sign into and nothing to sync, so most problems are solved on the device itself — see below.

Recording a price

From the Price Book tab, tap an item (or add one with the + button), then Record Price. Enter the price, confirm the store, package size and unit, and tap Check Price. PriceJar will show whether it is the best price you have on record, typical, or above your usual, and saving from that screen is what actually adds it to the item's history.

The scanner does not recognise a barcode

PriceJar's scanner only recalls barcodes you have already attached to one of your own items — it never looks a code up online. Scanning a new code opens Add Item with the code already filled in, so you only have to name the item once. If the camera itself will not open, check Settings → PriceJar → Camera on your iPhone; you can always add an item and its price by hand instead.

A price won't save

PriceJar keeps mass, volume and count measurements separate on purpose, since 500 g and 500 mL are never the same comparison. If a price is rejected, the unit you picked does not match the item's own measurement type — open the item, check its unit under Edit Item, and record the price again with a matching unit.

Removing the sample data

The twelve example items PriceJar can load on first run are always badged Sample and never count toward Stats or CSV exports. Go to Settings → Remove Sample Data to delete them in one tap; your own entries are never touched by this.

Exporting your data

Settings → Export CSV shares a spreadsheet of every item, store and price entry you have recorded, through the standard iOS share sheet, so you can save it to Files, email it, or open it in any spreadsheet app.

Starting over

Settings → Delete All Data permanently removes every item, price, store and list on this iPhone after you type DELETE to confirm. This cannot be undone, so export a CSV first if you want to keep a copy.

About the ads

PriceJar is free, funded by a small banner on the Price Book, Item History, Stores and Stats screens, and by at most one full-screen ad after a shopping trip is saved. No ad ever appears while you are entering a price, scanning, shopping, or looking at a verdict. Settings → Remove Ads turns every ad off permanently with a one-time purchase; Restore Purchases on the same screen recovers it after a reinstall or on a new device signed into the same Apple ID.
