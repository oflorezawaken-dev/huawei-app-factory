Translate Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026 (version 1.0)

Summary: The text you type is translated on your phone and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Translate needs no account and asks for no camera, microphone, photo, storage or location permission. It uses the network for exactly two things: downloading a language pack the first time you use a language pair, and showing ads. The translations you keep, and your settings, stay in the app's private storage on the device.

Your text is not sent anywhere

Translation runs entirely on the device, using a model that Translate downloads once and then uses offline. Nothing you type, paste or translate leaves your phone: there is no server of ours for it to reach, and the app makes no network request that carries it. That is why the app keeps working with the connection switched off once a language pair is downloaded.

Language packs

The first time you translate between two languages, Translate downloads that pair's model from Huawei. The request identifies the app and the pair being requested; it does not contain your text. The app is configured to use Huawei's Germany data-processing region for this. Downloaded packs can be deleted at any time on the Languages screen, which also frees the space they take.

What Translate stores on your phone

  • History: your last 100 translations, so one can be pulled back without retyping it. You can delete any entry, clear the lot, or switch history off entirely in Settings — switching it off also empties it.
  • Preferences: your theme choice, the last language pair you used, and which packs you have downloaded.

None of this is transmitted to us. There is no account, no sign-in and no profile of any kind. Translate sets allowBackup=false, so Android's device backup does not copy the app's data either.

Permissions

Translate declares two permissions, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. They exist for the language-pack download and for the advertising SDK. The app requests no camera, microphone, photo library, file storage, location, contacts or notification permission, and cannot use them.

Advertising (Huawei Petal Ads)

Translate is free and supported by advertising. Ads are served by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK. A banner appears on the Languages and Settings screens, and an ad may appear after a translation completes. No ad is shown on the screen where you are typing or reading a result.

To deliver and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (OAID), device model and OS version, network and carrier information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. This information is processed by Huawei under its own privacy policy; Translate itself does not receive or store it. The text you translate is never shared with the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization on Huawei devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy, and on other Android devices under the equivalent "Ads" setting. For Huawei's own disclosures, see the Petal Ads documentation.

How many languages

The list of languages comes from the on-device engine itself and was 55 on the device this version was tested with. Translate does not claim to translate every language, and the list in the app is always the one the engine actually supports rather than a number written into the app.

Data deletion

Clearing the history removes it from the device. Deleting a language pack removes its model. Uninstalling Translate removes all of the app's data, including every downloaded pack.

Children's privacy

Translate is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case we do not collect personal information on any server.

Changes to this policy

If what Translate collects or does changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version is made available.
