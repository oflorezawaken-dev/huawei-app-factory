Hashtags Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026 (version 1.0)

Summary: Hashtags does not require an account and sends nothing you type or copy to any server operated by us. The tag sets ship inside the app, and the sets you build, the tags you star and your copy history are stored in the app's private storage. The app requests no camera, photo, storage, location or notification permission. Hashtags is free and shows ads served by Huawei Petal Ads on the topics and settings screens and after you copy a set; the ad SDK uses the network and may collect device and usage data for ad delivery.

What Hashtags stores

  • Your own sets: the name you give them and the tags you put in them.
  • Favourites: the tags you star.
  • Copy history: the last ten copies, so the same combination can be pulled back without rebuilding it.
  • Preferences: your theme choice and whether sets open fully selected.

None of this is transmitted to us. We do not operate any server that receives it. There is no user account, sign-in, or profile of any kind. Hashtags sets allowBackup=false, so Android's device backup does not copy the app's data either.

The tag sets ship with the app

The topic sets are a file inside the installed app. Nothing is downloaded, and no request is made to fetch, rank or personalise them. That is why the whole app works with no network connection at all, and it is also why the app makes no claim about which tags are currently trending: it has no live data and does not pretend to.

Permissions

Hashtags declares two permissions, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and both exist only so the advertising SDK can load an ad. The app itself never uses the network. Hashtags does not request the camera, photo library, file storage, location, contacts or notification permissions, and cannot use them.

The clipboard

Copying puts the tags you selected on your device's clipboard. That is the point of the app. The clipboard is managed by Android, not by us; Hashtags only writes to it when you tap Copy, and never reads it.

Advertising (Huawei Petal Ads)

Hashtags is free and supported by advertising. Ads are served by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK. A banner appears on the topics and settings screens, and an ad may appear after you copy a set. No ad of any kind is shown on the screen where you are choosing tags.

To deliver and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (OAID), device model and OS version, network and carrier information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. This information is processed by Huawei under its own privacy policy; Hashtags itself does not receive or store it. The sets you build and the tags you copy are never shared with the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization on Huawei devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy, and on other Android devices under the equivalent "Ads" setting. For Huawei's own disclosures, see the Petal Ads documentation.

Data deletion

Deleting a set removes it and its tags from the device. Uninstalling Hashtags removes all of its data.

Children's privacy

Hashtags is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case we do not collect personal information on any server.

Changes to this policy

If what Hashtags collects or does changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version is made available.
