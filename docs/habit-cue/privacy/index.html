HabitCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026 (version 1.0)

Summary: HabitCue does not require an account and does not send your habits or completion history to any server operated by us. Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and the app does not back that data up to any cloud. HabitCue has no camera, no photos, no storage permission and no location access — it only ever handles the habit names and colours you type or pick, and the dates you mark done. HabitCue is free and shows ads served by Huawei Petal Ads on two screens; the ad SDK uses the network and may collect device and usage data for ad delivery.

What HabitCue stores

  • Your habits: the name and colour you give them, and the schedule you set (every day, specific weekdays, or a target number of times a week).
  • Completion history: the calendar date each time you mark a habit done, used to compute streaks and the monthly calendar.
  • Preferences: the reminder hour and your theme choice.

None of this is transmitted to us. We do not operate any server that receives it. There is no user account, sign-in, or profile of any kind. HabitCue sets allowBackup=false, so Android's device backup does not copy the app's data either.

Notifications

HabitCue asks for permission to post notifications so it can send one daily reminder listing the habits that are still undone. The reminder is generated on your device from your own schedules; no data leaves the device to produce it. You can turn it off any time in your system settings.

No camera, photos, storage or location

HabitCue does not request the camera, photo library, file storage or location permissions, and does not use them. The only thing you can attach to a habit is a name and a colour chosen from a fixed palette built into the app.

Advertising (Huawei Petal Ads)

HabitCue is free and supported by advertising. Ads are served by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK and appear only on the Habits and Stats screens — never on Today, while adding or editing a habit, or in a session opened from a reminder notification.

To deliver and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (OAID), device model and OS version, network and carrier information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. This information is processed by Huawei under its own privacy policy; HabitCue itself does not receive or store it. Your habit names and completion history are never shared with the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization on Huawei devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy, and on other Android devices under the equivalent "Ads" setting. For Huawei's own disclosures, see the Petal Ads documentation.

Data deletion

Deleting a habit removes its full completion history from the device. Uninstalling HabitCue removes all of its data.

Children's privacy

HabitCue is a general-purpose habit-tracking tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case we do not collect personal information on any server.

Changes to this policy

If what HabitCue collects or does changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version is made available.
