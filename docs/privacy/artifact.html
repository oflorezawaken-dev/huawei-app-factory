ReceiptLens

Privacy Policy

Last updated — 2026-09-06

In short: ReceiptLens does not require an account, does not send your receipts or receipt data to any server we operate, and does not currently show ads. Everything you scan or enter is stored in the app's private storage, on your device.

data stored

What ReceiptLens keeps

Receipt photos you capture or import, saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

Receipt data you enter or edit — merchant, date, amount, tax, currency, category — kept in a local database on your device.

Preferences, such as your default currency and display language.

None of this is transmitted to us. There is no account, sign-in, or profile of any kind.

camera

Camera permission

ReceiptLens asks for camera access only to photograph a receipt. The photo is processed and stored on your device; the app does not upload it anywhere.

on-device ocr

Text recognition

ReceiptLens is designed to read a merchant, date, and amount off a receipt photo using on-device text recognition, so you can review and correct the result before saving. This runs, or is intended to run, entirely on your device — no photo or extracted text is sent to a server for it. When recognition isn't available, ReceiptLens asks you to enter the receipt manually instead; your data doesn't leave the device either way.

advertising

Ads

This version of ReceiptLens shows no ads. A later version may add Huawei Petal Ads on a few non-critical screens, never during capture, review, or data entry. If we turn ads on, we will update this page first to describe what the ad SDK collects, matching Huawei's own AppGallery and Petal Ads disclosures.

export

Exporting your data

Settings lets you export your receipts to a CSV file. That file is built on your device and shared through whatever option you pick; we never receive a copy.

device backup

Device-level backups

ReceiptLens does not back up your data to any cloud service of ours. If your device's own backup feature is turned on — a Google or device-maker account backup, for instance — that system-level feature may include ReceiptLens' app data, the same way it can for any other app on your device. You can review and control that in your device's backup settings.

deletion

Deleting your data

You can delete any receipt from inside the app at any time. Uninstalling ReceiptLens removes everything it stored locally.

children

Children's privacy

ReceiptLens is a general-purpose expense tool, not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children — and as above, we don't collect personal information on any server at all.

changes

Changes to this policy

If what ReceiptLens collects or does changes — enabling ads or a cloud feature in a future version, for example — we'll update this page and the date above before that version ships.
