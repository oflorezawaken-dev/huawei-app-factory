ReceiptLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 (version 1.1)

Summary: ReceiptLens does not require an account and does not send your receipts or receipt data to any server operated by us. Everything you scan or enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app is free and shows ads served by Huawei Petal Ads on a few non-critical screens; the ad SDK uses the network and may collect device and usage data for ad delivery.

What ReceiptLens stores

  • Receipt photos you capture or import, saved only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Receipt data you enter or edit — merchant name, date, amounts, currency, category — stored in a local database on your device.
  • App preferences, such as your default currency and display language.

None of this information is transmitted to us, and we do not operate any server that receives it. There is no user account, sign-in, or profile of any kind.

Camera permission

ReceiptLens requests camera access solely to let you photograph a receipt. The photo is processed and stored on your device; it is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Text recognition (OCR)

ReceiptLens is designed to extract merchant, date, and amount suggestions from a receipt photo using on-device text recognition, so you can review and correct them before saving. Text recognition runs, or is intended to run, entirely on your device — no receipt image or extracted text is sent to a server for this purpose. If recognition is unavailable on your device, ReceiptLens asks you to enter the details manually; no data leaves your device either way.

Advertising (Huawei Petal Ads)

ReceiptLens is free and supported by advertising. Ads are served by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK and appear only on the History, Statistics, and Settings screens — never while you capture, review, or enter a receipt.

To deliver and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (OAID), device model and OS version, network and carrier information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. This information is processed by Huawei under its own privacy policy; ReceiptLens itself does not receive or store it. Your receipt photos, text, and expense records are never shared with the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization on Huawei devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy, and on other Android devices under Settings → Google → Ads (or the equivalent "Ads" setting for your device). For Huawei's own disclosures, see the Petal Ads privacy statement linked from Huawei Petal Ads documentation.

Exporting your data

You can export your receipts to a CSV file from Settings. That file is created on your device and shared using the share options you choose; we do not receive a copy.

Device backups

ReceiptLens does not back up your data to any cloud service that we control. If your device's operating system has its own backup feature enabled (for example, a Google or device-manufacturer account backup), that system-level feature may include ReceiptLens' app data the same way it can for other apps on your device. You can review and control this in your device's own backup settings.

Data deletion

You can delete individual receipts at any time from within the app. Uninstalling ReceiptLens removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

Children's privacy

ReceiptLens is a general-purpose expense-tracking tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case we do not collect personal information on any server, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change what ReceiptLens collects or how it behaves — for example, when enabling ads or a cloud feature in a future version — we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version is made available.
