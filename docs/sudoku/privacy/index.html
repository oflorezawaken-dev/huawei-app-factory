Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026 (version 1.0)

Summary: Sudoku does not require an account and sends nothing you do in the game to any server operated by us. Puzzles are generated on your own device, and your game in progress, your times and your statistics are stored in the app's private storage. The app requests no camera, photo, storage, location or notification permission. Sudoku is free and shows ads served by Huawei Petal Ads on the home and statistics screens and at the end of a puzzle; the ad SDK uses the network and may collect device and usage data for ad delivery.

What Sudoku stores

  • The game in progress: the puzzle, its solution, the numbers and pencil notes you have entered, the elapsed time, and how many mistakes and hints you have used. There is at most one such game at a time; starting a new puzzle replaces it.
  • Finished games: for each game, the difficulty, whether you solved it, how long it took, and how many mistakes and hints it took. This is what the statistics screen reads.
  • Preferences: your theme choice and whether highlights, the mistake limit and the timer are on.

None of this is transmitted to us. We do not operate any server that receives it. There is no user account, sign-in, or profile of any kind. Sudoku sets allowBackup=false, so Android's device backup does not copy the app's data either.

Puzzles are generated on your device

Every puzzle is built by the app itself: it constructs a complete grid, then removes clues while checking that exactly one solution remains. No puzzle is downloaded, and no request is made to fetch, verify or score one. That is why the whole game works with no network connection at all.

Permissions

Sudoku declares two permissions, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and both exist only so the advertising SDK can load an ad. The game itself never uses the network. Sudoku does not request the camera, photo library, file storage, location, contacts or notification permissions, and cannot use them.

Advertising (Huawei Petal Ads)

Sudoku is free and supported by advertising. Ads are served by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK. A banner appears on the home and statistics screens, and an ad may appear when a puzzle ends. No ad of any kind is ever shown on the board while you are playing.

To deliver and measure ads, the Petal Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (OAID), device model and OS version, network and carrier information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. This information is processed by Huawei under its own privacy policy; Sudoku itself does not receive or store it. Your games, times and statistics are never shared with the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization on Huawei devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy, and on other Android devices under the equivalent "Ads" setting. For Huawei's own disclosures, see the Petal Ads documentation.

Data deletion

Starting a new puzzle discards the previous game in progress. Uninstalling Sudoku removes all of its data from the device.

Children's privacy

Sudoku is a general-purpose puzzle game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case we do not collect personal information on any server.

Changes to this policy

If what Sudoku collects or does changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version is made available.
