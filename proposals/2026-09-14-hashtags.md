# Proposal: Hashtags — rebuilding the app with the traffic and no conversion

## Why this one

Of the owner's apps, this is the strangest number in the table (2026-03-01 to
2026-08-28):

| App | Impressions | CTR | Conversion | Downloads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Translate Everything | 245,518 | 4.26% | 30.58% | 5,114 |
| **HashTags for Instagram** | **195,198** | **0.46%** | **3.85%** | **127** |
| All GTA Cheats Codes | 114,858 | 4.01% | 6.07% | 970 |
| Sudoku - Sudoku Puzzles | 108,967 | 4.85% | 48.05% | 3,177 |

Nearly two hundred thousand people were shown this app and **fewer than one in
two hundred tapped it**. Every other app in the portfolio converts eight to ten
times better at the same step. The traffic is already there and already free;
what fails is everything after the impression.

That makes it the cheapest possible win. Sudoku needed a whole game engine to
capture demand that was already converting. This one needs a listing that does
not repel people who are actively looking at it.

Two things plausibly explain a 0.46% CTR, and we can fix both:

- **The name carries someone else's trademark.** "HashTags for Instagram" reads
  as an unofficial third-party add-on, which is exactly what a cautious user
  scrolls past — and it is a trademark risk regardless, now banned by factory
  rule 10.
- **The icon and listing were never designed.** At the impression stage, the
  icon and the first line are the whole product.

The rebuild is also necessary for the same reason Sudoku was: the keystore is
lost and App Signing was never enabled, so the published app can never be
updated and will be delisted when AppGallery raises the minimum target API.

Query captured: **hashtags** (plus "tags", "etiquetas").

| it-IT | Hashtags - Tag per i tuoi Post |

The keyword leads in every language and **no platform is named anywhere** — not
in the store name, not in the listing, not in the app. The app is about
hashtags, which belong to no one; it is not "for" any company's product.

## What the app does

A hashtag toolkit that works entirely offline:

- **Curated sets by topic** — travel, food, fitness, photography, art, business,
  music, nature, fashion, pets, and so on. Each set is a hand-built group of
  tags that actually belong together, not a scrape.
- **Copy in one tap**, the whole set or individual tags, with a counter, because
  most platforms cap a post at 30 tags and people hit that limit blind.
- **Build your own sets** and keep them, so the tool earns a second use.
- **Search** across every tag and set.
- **Favourites**, and a history of what was copied recently.

Deliberately not in V1: anything that claims to know what is trending. That
would need a live feed, it would be wrong within a week, and the honest version
of it is a lie in a store listing — the factory has a rule about that.

## Candidate scoring

| Candidate | Search demand | Build ease | Ad fit | Low risk | Upside | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hashtags** | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **23** |
| Weather (Clima) | 4 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 15 |
| Translator | 5 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 17 |

Weather needs a data provider whose free tier permits commercial, ad-supported
use, which is an open legal question rather than an engineering one. Translation
needs real on-device ML Kit, the largest integration risk available. Hashtags
needs a content file and good taste.

The upside column is doing real work here: the other two would start from zero
impressions, while this one starts from 195,198 that already exist.

## Ads placement

Banner on the topic list and on Settings. Interstitial after a set is copied —
a genuine end-of-task moment — under the existing frequency policy. No ad on the
screen where someone is selecting tags.

## Permissions and privacy

`INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, for Petal Ads and nothing else. No
camera, no storage, no location, no notifications, no accounts. Everything the
user makes stays in the app's private database.

## Open risks

- **Content quality is the product.** A scraped tag dump would be worthless and
  obvious. The sets have to be written with judgement, which is the real work
  here and the part that cannot be generated carelessly.
- **The 0.46% may not be the name.** It is the best hypothesis, not a proven
  cause; the icon may matter more. Both get fixed, and the funnel is measurable
  afterwards, so the next iteration has data instead of theories.
- **Category crowding.** There are many hashtag apps. The defence is the same as
  Sudoku's: exact query match, an honest listing, and no paywall.
