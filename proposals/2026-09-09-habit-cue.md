# Proposal: HabitCue — an honest daily habit tracker

## Pitch

HabitCue is an offline daily habit tracker: add a habit, pick how often it repeats, tap
it done, keep a streak. It has no account, no cloud sync, and — unlike almost every
top habit tracker — no paywall hiding the features that make tracking actually work.
The target user is anyone trying to build a handful of daily routines (exercise, reading,
water, meditation, no-phone-before-bed) who has tried Habitify, Streaks, or HabitNow and
hit a wall where recurring schedules, more than a few habits, or basic stats sit behind a
subscription. HabitCue is free, supported by Huawei Petal Ads, and keeps the entire
feature set unlocked from day one.

## 5-candidate scoring (1-5 each; demand/competition-weakness/build-ease/ad-friendliness/
low-risk — higher is always better)

| Candidate | Demand | Competitor weakness | Build ease | Ad-friendliness | Low risk | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HabitCue (habit tracker)** | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **22** |
| Unit & measurement converter | 5 | 3 | 5 | 2 | 5 | 20 |
| Hydration / water reminder | 3 | 1 | 5 | 3 | 5 | 17 |
| Manual budget / expense log | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 17 |
| Warranty / subscription expiry tracker | 3 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 16 |

Notes on the runners-up:
- **Unit converter**: huge search demand, but the honest-and-free niche is already served
  by small dedicated apps ("no ads, no trackers"), and a converter used for a few seconds
  at a time has almost no natural pause for an ad — forcing one in risks recreating the
  exact "ads interrupt every conversion" complaint the research surfaced. Low ad-friendliness
  sinks it under our own must-show-ads rule.
- **Manual budget log**: reasonable demand, but it overlaps in spirit with ReceiptLens
  (already in `factory/apps.json`), which the research constraints rule out.
- **Warranty tracker**: smaller search volume, and the top free Android competitors are
  already offline, already free, and already require no account — leaving little room to
  differentiate.
- **Hydration reminder**: near-commodity; too saturated to stand out.

## Top competitors (with what they do badly)

- **Habitify** — 4.6★ App Store / 4.4★ Play (Aug 2026). Recurring tasks, calendar sync
  and "AI planning" all sit behind Pro; reviewers say the free tier "feels like a teaser."
  ([makeheadway.com, 2026](https://makeheadway.com/blog/habitify/))
- **Several top-10 trackers** (per a 2026 comparison roundup) — "habit trackers have some
  of the most aggressive paywalls in the app store, and the paywall usually lands on the
  exact features that make tracking work." Billing complaints about surprise renewals
  after a free trial are a recurring theme. ([apprundown.com, 2026](https://apprundown.com/best/habit-tracker-apps))
- **EasyHabits** (iPhone) — praised as free, but caps free users at **3 habits**.
  ([easyhabits.io, 2026](https://www.easyhabits.io/blog/best-free-habit-tracker))
- **TickTick** — combines tasks + habits, but only **5 habits** free.
  ([easyhabits.io, 2026](https://www.easyhabits.io/blog/best-free-habit-tracker))
- **Loop Habit Tracker** — the one honestly-free exception found (no ads, no Pro tier,
  no account) — proof the "just give people the whole thing" positioning works, but it is
  Google Play / GMS-oriented and does not reach AppGallery-first Huawei users.
  ([easyhabits.io, 2026](https://www.easyhabits.io/blog/best-free-habit-tracker))

The gap: nothing found combines *unlimited habits, no account, no paywall* **and**
distribution to Huawei AppGallery users without Google services.

## V1 feature list (buildable in a week, same stack as ReceiptLens/PlantCue)

- Add habit: name, colour/icon, schedule (every day / specific weekdays / N times a week).
- Today view: habits due today, grouped, one-tap Done, undo.
- Streak per habit: current streak and longest streak, computed from history.
- All-habits list with next-due state; edit and delete (delete removes its history).
- Simple monthly calendar per habit showing completed/missed days.
- Stats screen: overall completion rate, best streak across habits, total completions.
- One daily reminder notification (WorkManager) at a user-chosen hour, only when
  something is still undone; tapping opens Today.
- Settings: reminder hour, light/dark/system theme, privacy info, ads info.
- Huawei Petal Ads: banner on the All-habits and Stats screens; interstitial on opening
  Stats, gated by the same frequency policy already proven in PlantCue (warm-up delay,
  minimum interval, capped frequency). Never on Today's checklist or Add/Edit habit.
- 9 factory languages, RTL for Arabic, accessibility (content descriptions, 48dp targets).

### Not in V1

Habit categories/tags beyond colour, quit/negative habits, per-habit custom reminder
times, home-screen widgets, calendar app sync, social sharing or friend accountability,
any AI-generated suggestions, multi-device sync.

## Ads placement

Banner: All-habits list, Stats screen. Interstitial: opening Stats only, at most once
per the existing warm-up/spacing policy. Never on Today (the daily checklist), never on
Add/Edit habit, never triggered by marking a habit done — the exact places where the
competitor research shows ads driving users away.

## Permissions and privacy (two sentences)

HabitCue asks only for `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (the daily reminder) and `INTERNET` /
`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (Petal Ads only) — no camera, no photos, no storage, no location,
the smallest permission footprint of any factory app so far. All habits and history stay
in the device's private database with no account and no cloud sync; only the Petal Ads
SDK touches the network, and that is declared honestly in AppGallery's privacy tags from
day one, learned directly from the ReceiptLens and PlantCue rejections.

## Open risks

- Habit tracking is a crowded category; differentiation rests entirely on actually
  shipping the full feature set free, with no paywall ever added later — that has to stay
  true, or the app becomes "one more me-too tracker."
- The research shows users abandon utility apps over intrusive ads (unit-converter
  complaints); HabitCue must keep ads off the daily-use screens (Today) exactly as
  PlantCue kept them off Add/Edit and reminder-triggered opens.
- "HabitCue" has not been checked against existing AppGallery/Play listings for a name
  clash; do that at the spec/generate step before committing to store assets.
