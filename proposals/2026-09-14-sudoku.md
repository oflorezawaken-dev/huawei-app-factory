# Proposal: Sudoku — rebuilding the owner's best-performing app

## Why this one, and why now

This is not a speculative market bet. It is a rebuild of the app with the best funnel in
the owner's own AppGallery portfolio, using first-party numbers from 2026-03-01 to
2026-08-28:

| App | Impressions | CTR | Conversion | Downloads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Translate Everything | 245,518 | 4.26% | 30.58% | 5,114 |
| HashTags for Instagram | 195,198 | 0.46% | 3.85% | 127 |
| All GTA Cheats Codes | 114,858 | 4.01% | 6.07% | 970 |
| Guide to Watch Football | 114,502 | 2.32% | 17.84% | 754 |
| **Sudoku - Sudoku Puzzles** | **108,967** | **4.85%** | **48.05%** | **3,177** |
| How to recover deleted messages | 77,047 | 4.02% | 25.15% | 1,268 |
| Clima - Weather Forecast | 63,770 | 4.75% | 27.47% | 1,140 |

Sudoku has the **best click-through rate and by far the best conversion rate** in the
portfolio. Nearly half of everyone who opens its store page installs it, because the
query and the product are the same thing: someone who searches "sudoku" wants sudoku.

The rebuild is necessary, not cosmetic. The existing app was signed with a keystore that
has been lost and was never enrolled in AppGallery App Signing, so it can never be
updated again — Huawei does not allow changing a signature key, and enrolling a released
app requires the very key that is gone. AppGallery raises its minimum target API level
over time, so an app that cannot be updated will eventually be delisted on its own. The
install base is on borrowed time regardless of what we do.

## Candidate scoring

Rebuild candidates were the owner's own apps, scored 1-5 (higher is better):

| Candidate | Search demand | Build ease | Ad fit | Low risk | Proven conversion | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sudoku** | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **25** |
| Hashtags | 5 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 17 |
| Weather (Clima) | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 16 |
| Translator | 5 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 16 |
| GTA cheats / football guide | 4 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 13 |

Notes on the others: hashtags is trivially cheap and has huge impressions but a broken
0.46% CTR that has to be fixed with icon and naming, and it must never mention Instagram.
Weather needs a data provider whose free tier permits commercial, ad-supported use — an
open question, so it is not first. Translation needs real on-device Huawei ML Kit, which
is the largest integration risk in the list. Cheat-code and branded-guide apps are now
banned by factory rule: someone else's trademark and the worst conversion in the data.

## V1 feature list (buildable in a week)

- Puzzle generator on device: valid grid, cells removed while a **unique solution** is
  preserved, four difficulties by number of givens (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert).
- Board: select a cell, enter 1-9, erase, pencil notes mode, undo.
- Assistance: highlight the selected row/column/box, highlight identical numbers, flag
  conflicting entries, limited hints (3 per puzzle).
- Timer, pause, auto-save, resume the game in progress after closing the app.
- Win screen with difficulty and time.
- Statistics per difficulty: played, won, best time, average time, current and longest
  win streak.
- Settings: theme (light/dark/system), highlights on/off, mistake limit on/off, timer
  on/off.
- All 9 factory languages, RTL for Arabic, accessibility.
- Petal Ads: banner on Home and Statistics; interstitial **after a puzzle ends**, under
  the existing frequency policy.

### Not in V1

Daily challenge and calendar, online leaderboards, accounts, cloud sync, killer/6x6/16x16
variants, themes beyond light and dark, sound effects, rewarded-ad hints, notifications.

## Ads placement

Banner on Home and Statistics only. Interstitial only when a puzzle ends (solved or
abandoned), never mid-board, never on the board screen at all. This is the cleanest ad
surface the factory has ever had: the end of a round is a genuine pause, which is exactly
where the frequency policy in `PetalAdsManager` was designed to fire.

## Permissions and privacy

`INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, for Petal Ads and nothing else. No camera, no
storage, no location, **no notifications** — the smallest permission footprint of any
factory app so far, smaller even than HabitCue. Everything the player produces (progress,
times, stats) stays in the app's private database, and the AppGallery privacy tags carry
only the standard Petal Ads block.

## Open risks

- **Generator performance.** Uniqueness checking while removing cells is the expensive
  part; it must run off the main thread with a visible loading state, and Expert
  difficulty must be measured on a real device, not only on the emulator.
- **Search cannibalisation.** The owner's existing Sudoku stays live and keeps taking
  impressions. Plan: leave it published until the new one gains traction, then take the
  old one off sale.
- **Category crowding.** Sudoku is a crowded category everywhere; the defence is the
  same one the existing app already proves works — exact query match, honest listing, and
  a build that is fast and free with no paywall.
