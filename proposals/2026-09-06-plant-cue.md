# Proposal: PlantCue — offline plant-care reminders

**Date:** 2026-09-06 · **Slug:** `plant-cue` · **Package:** `com.huaweiappfactory.plantcue`

## Pitch

PlantCue is a plant-care reminder app for people who keep killing their houseplants. You add a
plant (name, optional photo, room), set how often it needs water and, optionally, fertiliser or
misting, and PlantCue reminds you with a local notification. Everything lives on the device: no
account, no cloud, no paywall, no "AI identification" upsell. Free, supported by Huawei Petal Ads
on the overview and stats screens only.

**Target user:** someone with 3–30 houseplants who wants a dependable reminder without a subscription
or an account, on a Huawei phone or any Android.

## Why this category (evidence, dated 2026)

- The category's leading apps monetise through aggressive trials and paywalls: PictureThis'
  7-day trial auto-converts to an annual charge and its reviews are full of billing complaints;
  Planta and PictureThis both sit behind trial paywalls
  ([garden.gg, 2026](https://garden.gg/blog/best-free-plant-care-apps-2026/),
  [AI Tool Giant, 2026](https://www.aitoolgiant.com/reviews/best-ai-plant-care-apps-2026.html)).
- Free tiers that don't expire exist (Garden.gg, Greg, Plantix) but they are AI/identification
  products that need network and accounts; plain "remind me to water" without sign-up is the
  underserved middle ([MobileAppDaily, 2026](https://www.mobileappdaily.com/products/free-plant-care-apps)).
- Huawei devices without Google services are poorly served by the big players, whose Android
  builds lean on GMS for push and billing; a local-notification, HMS-friendly app has a clear seat
  ([smiletotalk, 2025](https://www.smiletotalk.com/blog/huawei-app-alternatives-how-to-use-your-device-without-google)).

## V1 feature list (one week)

- Add / edit / delete plants: name, optional photo (camera or gallery), room/tag, notes.
- Per-plant schedules: water every N days; optional fertilise every N weeks; optional mist.
- Local notifications at a user-chosen daily hour; "Done" and "Snooze 1 day" actions.
- Today view: what needs care today and what's overdue; one-tap "watered".
- Plant list with photo thumbnails, filter by room, sort by next due.
- Care history per plant (date, action) and simple stats (streak, plants on time this month).
- Light / dark / system theme; 9 store languages.
- Petal Ads: banner on the plant list and stats screens; interstitial at most once per session
  when opening stats; never on add/edit/photo screens or from a notification tap.

**Not in V1:** plant identification, disease diagnosis, weather/light sensors, cloud sync,
sharing, widgets, wear OS.

## Permissions and privacy story

`CAMERA` (optional photo), `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (reminders), `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` only if
needed for on-time reminders (prefer inexact WorkManager), `INTERNET` + `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`
(ads only). Plants, photos and history stay in app-private storage; the only network user is
the Petal Ads SDK, disclosed in the privacy policy.

## Open risks

- Name collision: "PlantCue" must be checked in AppGallery/Play before creating the app; fallback
  names: "LeafCue", "WaterWhen".
- Notification reliability on aggressive battery managers (EMUI): document the battery
  whitelist step in-app.
- Petal Ads fill outside Huawei devices is low; revenue depends on the HMS install base.
- Machine-drafted translations need a native review before launch, same as ReceiptLens.
